To Be Free

Trace your life's path — walk to clear the world's fog

Download on the App Store

What it is

When you first open To Be Free, the entire world is hidden beneath dark fog. Only the places you physically walk to are cleared, revealing the map beneath. Your own two feet are the key that opens the world.

Features

  • Real-time GPS fog clearing — just walk, and it records automatically
  • Three difficulty levels with different clearing radii
  • Satellite map, border and coastline layers
  • Global leaderboard — compete by how much of the planet you've uncovered
  • Cloud backup so your exploration survives a device change
  • Share your exploration map as an image
